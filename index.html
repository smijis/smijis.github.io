Hello, my name is Selina.

I'm a clinical psychologist taking the leap into coding. Since I was younger, I enjoyed art and design. My dream is to build user-friendly, aesthetic apps that make mental health care more accessible, affordable, and effective for Australians. Outside of work and coding, you'll find me bouldering, playing my flute, and spending time with my friends.

Join me as my worlds of psychology and technology collide.
